# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 22 October 2026*

Rydd is a macOS menu bar app that keeps the folders you choose tidy, made by TangSpark. This policy covers every version of Rydd: the Mac App Store version and the one downloaded from tang-spark.no.

**Rydd collects no data.** It has no network access at all; the operating system's App Sandbox does not allow it to connect to the internet, and its code contains no networking. There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or update checks, and no third-party code.

What Rydd does on your Mac:

- It uses only the folders you allow in an Open panel (and Downloads), and tidies only the ones you choose, such as Downloads and Desktop.
- It reads the names of the files there, and basic details such as their size, dates and the website a download came from.
- To know where files belong, it reads the names of your folders and of the files inside them.
- If you turn on **Sort with Apple Intelligence**, file and folder names are given to Apple's on-device language model on your Mac. Rydd never uses Apple's cloud model. This works offline.
- File contents are not read unless you turn on **Read File Contents**. Then Rydd reads a short excerpt of a document on your Mac, only when its name isn't enough, keeps it in memory while sorting and never saves it.
- It moves files only after you confirm. "Move to Trash" uses the macOS Trash. Every tidy can be undone.
- In its own container (`~/Library/Containers/com.feiyangtang.Rydd`) it keeps your settings, the folders you allowed, short descriptions of your folders made from their names, and the list of recent moves so they can be undone.

None of this leaves your Mac. Deleting Rydd and its container removes everything it stored.

Rydd is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children. Questions: [support@tang-spark.no](mailto:support@tang-spark.no).
