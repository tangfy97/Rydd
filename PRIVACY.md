# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 22 October 2026*

Rydd is a macOS menu bar app that keeps the folders you choose tidy, made by TangSpark.

**Rydd collects no data.** It contains no networking code and never connects to the internet. There are no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or update checks, and no third-party code.

What Rydd does on your Mac:

- It reads the names of the files in the folders you choose (for example Downloads and Desktop), and basic details such as their size, dates and the website a download came from.
- To know where files belong, it reads the names of your folders in Documents, Desktop, Pictures, Movies, Music and iCloud Drive, and the names of the files inside them.
- If you turn on **Sort with Apple Intelligence**, file and folder names are passed to Apple's on-device language model on your Mac. Rydd never uses Apple's cloud model. This works offline.
- File contents are not read unless you turn on **Read File Contents**. Then Rydd reads a short excerpt of a document on your Mac, only when its name isn't enough, keeps it in memory while sorting and never saves it.
- It moves files only after you confirm. "Move to Trash" uses the macOS Trash. Every tidy can be undone.
- It keeps two things in `~/Library/Application Support/Rydd`: short descriptions of your folders (made from names, so they don't have to be worked out again), the list of recent moves (so they can be undone). Your settings are stored in the usual macOS preferences.

None of this leaves your Mac. Deleting Rydd and that folder removes everything it stored.
